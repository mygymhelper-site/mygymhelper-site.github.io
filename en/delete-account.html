Deleting your MyGymHelper account

A MyGymHelper account is optional: if you use the app without signing in, we hold no data about you on our server.

From the app (recommended)

  • Open MyGymHelper and go to My area > Account.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account and the related server data are deleted immediately: profile, exercise lists and licence status. Data stored on your phone (workouts, measurements, settings) stays until you delete it from My area > Delete all local data or uninstall the app.

If you no longer have the app

Write to mygymhelper@gmail.com from the e-mail address linked to your account (the Google or Apple account you signed in with), with the subject "Account deletion". We will delete the account and the related data within 30 days and confirm the deletion to you.

Subscriptions

Deleting the account does not cancel your subscription, which is managed by the store:

  • Android: Google Play Store > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  • iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

Data we do not keep

We keep neither payment data nor health data: they stay with the store and on your phone respectively. For more details read the privacy policy: https://mygymhelper-site.github.io/en/privacy.html
