Disclaimer – fitness, nutrition, health and artificial intelligence

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Purpose of the application

The application MyGymHelper is designed to provide tools and information supporting personal wellbeing, physical activity, training, diet and nutrition, also by means of artificial intelligence systems running locally on the user's device.

The content is provided for information, education and general wellbeing support. Unless expressly stated and compliant with the applicable regulatory requirements, the application is not a health service, a medical service or individual professional advice.

2. The application does not replace professionals

The application in no way replaces the opinion, diagnosis, assessment, treatment or care of a physician, a medical specialist, a dietitian, a nutritionist or a qualified personal trainer, each within their own competence.

No content should be interpreted as a diagnosis, a medical prescription, a therapy or an authorisation to change or stop prescribed treatments.

Before starting a new training programme, significantly changing your diet or taking supplements, you should consult the competent professional, especially in case of illness, injury, allergies, pregnancy, drug therapies or other conditions that may affect your health.

3. Limits of artificial intelligence

The application uses artificial intelligence systems to process the available information and generate suggestions about physical activity, diet and wellbeing.

Answers are generated automatically and may contain errors, inaccuracies, omissions or indications that are not appropriate to the individual situation.

Artificial intelligence does not necessarily have all the information needed to assess the user's condition, does not perform a medical examination and does not guarantee the accuracy or safety of every suggestion.

Personalised answers are not equivalent to a clinical assessment or to individual professional advice. The user should check with a qualified professional any indication that could significantly affect their health.

4. Safety during physical activity

The proposed exercises and programmes must be adapted to the user's abilities, experience and individual condition.

The user must use equipment correctly, respect their own limits and stop the activity in case of pain, dizziness, unusual breathing difficulties or other unusual symptoms.

In case of persistent or worrying symptoms, contact a healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact the emergency services.

The application does not guarantee the absence of injuries or specific physical or sporting results.

5. Safety of dietary suggestions

Dietary suggestions are meant to support general wellbeing and do not guarantee that a diet is adequate for individual needs.

The application must not be used as a substitute for a professional meal plan, in particular in case of illness, eating disorders, allergies, intolerances, pregnancy, surgery or special nutritional needs.

The information generated must not be used to change therapies, drug dosages or prescribed treatments on your own.

6. Local operation of artificial intelligence

The application is designed to run the artificial intelligence features described as local on the user's device.

When those features actually run locally, the information used for processing is not sent to an external server to run the model.

This statement is limited to the features actually implemented and verified. Any separate services, such as updates, statistics, advertising, crash reporting, remote assistants or other integrations, must be described separately in the privacy policy, if present.

8. No emergency service

The application is not an emergency service, does not provide real-time health monitoring and does not ensure the intervention of a professional.
