Privacy policy and health data notice

Data controller: Nicola Gabriele Almeno

Privacy contact: mygymhelper@gmail.com

Application: MyGymHelper

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. What data is processed

The application may allow the user to enter and store on their own device:

  • Physical data, such as weight, height and training goals.
  • Information about physical activity and eating habits.
  • Health information entered voluntarily, such as declared conditions, allergies, intolerances and physical limitations.
  • Personal preferences needed to personalise the app's features.

Only the data actually required by the features in use will be processed.

2. Local storage

Personal and health data managed by the application's local features are designed to be stored on the user's device, without synchronisation with servers or cloud services run by the app.

The application does not send health data to a remote server for artificial intelligence processing, as long as the model and all relevant processing actually run locally.

This description is not a guarantee against every possible access to the data by third parties. The operating system, backup features, other installed applications or any external technical components may behave independently and must be verified.

3. Purposes of processing

The data entered may be used locally to:

  • Store the user's progress.
  • Personalise information and suggestions.
  • Prepare training programmes and indications.
  • Organise dietary information and personal goals.
  • Allow the on-device artificial intelligence features to work.

The data must not be used for further purposes incompatible with those communicated to the user.

4. Local artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence features described as local process information on the user's device.

The application does not send health data to external artificial intelligence providers for such processing.

Any additional features involving data transfers will be described separately and, where necessary, made available only after providing the information and obtaining the required permissions.

5. Access to data and disclosure to third parties

The controller does not automatically receive health data stored exclusively on the device through the local features alone.

Any data processed through separate services, such as purchases, statistics, technical diagnostics or support, will be handled according to their respective purposes and the applicable notice.

No absolute guarantees of no access by third parties will be stated without a technical check of the application and of the components used.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user or removed according to the actual operation of the application and of the operating system.

The user must be able to find the available ways to delete the data stored by the app. Uninstalling the app, resetting the device or other operating system operations may cause the loss of the information.

7. Security

The application must adopt security measures appropriate to the nature of the data processed, including, where appropriate, protection against unauthorised access, secure storage and data minimisation.

Actual security also depends on the device, the operating system, its configuration and the measures implemented.

8. Rights of the data subject

Where the processing falls within the scope of the GDPR, the data subject may exercise, under the conditions laid down by law, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and may withdraw consent when the processing is based on it.

To exercise the rights relating to processing carried out by the controller, contact: mygymhelper@gmail.com.

Data stored only locally may not be technically accessible to the controller. The ways of exercising rights must therefore be defined according to the processing actually carried out.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated in case of changes to the features, to the processing methods or to legal obligations.

Substantial changes will be communicated as required by the applicable law.

10. Account and purchases

The account is optional. If the user creates one, the application processes the data needed to sign in: e-mail address or identifier provided by the sign-in service, display name and language. Sign-in uses the Google account or, in the iPhone app, Sign in with Apple.

Account and database are managed by Supabase Inc., acting as processor on behalf of the controller. Data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Any transfer outside the European Union takes place with the safeguards provided by the supplier (standard contractual clauses).

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play in the Android app and by Apple (App Store) in the iPhone app. The controller receives the subscription status (and, if the user has an account, the link between purchase and account), not payment data. The controller may also assign a free licence to an account, recorded in the same database.

11. System health data and account deletion

  • Android – Health Connect: if the user connects Health Connect from My area, the application reads weight and height only when the user asks and writes completed strength workouts to Health Connect. Permissions are managed and revoked in the Health Connect settings; "Delete all data" also deletes the workouts the application wrote to Health Connect.
  • iPhone – Apple Health: if the user connects Apple Health from My area, the application reads weight and height only when the user asks and writes completed strength workouts to Health. Data written to Health remains managed by Apple Health and is deleted from the Health app.

In both cases the exchange happens on the device; the controller does not receive this data, which is used only for the features described and is not transferred to third parties or used for advertising.

A user who has created an account can delete it at any time from the app (My area > Account): the account and the related server data (profile, lists, licence status) are deleted. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately: in the Play Store (Payments & subscriptions) on Android, in the Apple ID settings on iPhone. Anyone who no longer has the app can request deletion by writing to mygymhelper@gmail.com from the address linked to the account, as explained at https://mygymhelper-site.github.io/en/delete-account.html.
